Privacy policy - homelab-rclone

homelab-rclone is used only by its owner. It asks for the Google Drive scope drive.file, so it can reach only the files it creates itself.

Those files are encrypted backups of the owner's own home server. They are stored in the owner's own Google Drive.

The app does not collect, share or sell data about anyone, and it is not used by third parties.

Questions: open an issue in this GitHub repository.
